Six Types of Healthcare Robots

Healthcare robots are designed for specific tasks, ranging from surgery and rehabilitation to delivery and communication.

01

Surgical Robots

Assist surgeons during complex procedures by providing precise movements and enhanced control.

02

Rehabilitation Robots

Help patients regain movement, strength and coordination during rehabilitation.

03

Pharmacy Robots

Help organise, store and distribute medicines accurately and efficiently.

04

Delivery Robots

Transport medicines, medical supplies and other materials around healthcare facilities.

05

Telepresence Robots

Allow doctors and healthcare professionals to communicate with patients from a distance.

06

Social Robots

Interact with patients and provide communication, reminders and basic assistance.
