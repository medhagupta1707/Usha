ROBOTICS × HEALTHCARE

About Robotics

Discover how robotics combines engineering, artificial intelligence and medicine to transform modern healthcare.

01 — THE BASICS

What is Robotics?

Robotics is a field of technology that deals with the design, construction, programming and operation of robots.

Robots can sense their surroundings, process information and perform tasks with a high level of accuracy.

When robotics is combined with healthcare, it can assist doctors, nurses, therapists and patients in a variety of medical tasks.

02 — THE CONNECTION

Robotics in Healthcare

Robots are becoming valuable assistants in hospitals, laboratories, pharmacies and rehabilitation centres.
01

Precision

Robotic systems can help perform highly precise movements during certain medical procedures.

02

Assistance

Robots can assist healthcare professionals with repetitive or physically demanding tasks.

03

Efficiency

Automation can help hospitals manage certain routine tasks more efficiently.

04

Accessibility

Telepresence and assistive robots can help provide healthcare support across distances.

04 — INSIDE A ROBOT

Key Components

Sensors

Help robots detect movement, position, temperature, pressure and their surroundings.

AI & Software

Enables robots to process information and perform programmed tasks.

Motors

Convert electrical energy into movement and allow robotic parts to move.

Control System

Coordinates the different components of a robot and controls its actions.
