04 — REAL WORLD IMPACT

Robotics in
Healthcare

From the operating room to patient rehabilitation, robotics is helping healthcare professionals deliver more precise, efficient and accessible care.

WHERE ROBOTS MAKE A DIFFERENCE

Applications of Healthcare Robotics

Robots can support healthcare professionals in medical procedures, patient care, logistics, rehabilitation and many other areas.

01

Robotic Surgery

Robotic systems can assist surgeons during certain procedures by providing precise movements, improved control and enhanced visualization.

SURGERY
02

Rehabilitation

Robotic systems can support physical therapy by helping patients practise controlled and repetitive movements.

RECOVERY
03

Diagnosis & Monitoring

Robotic and automated systems can assist with monitoring patients and collecting information that healthcare professionals can use.

DIAGNOSIS
04

Medicine Management

Robots can help organise, transport and distribute medicines within hospitals and pharmacies.

PHARMACY
05

Hospital Logistics

Autonomous robots can transport supplies, equipment and other materials between different areas of a healthcare facility.

LOGISTICS
06

Telemedicine

Telepresence robots can help healthcare professionals communicate with and interact with patients remotely.

REMOTE CARE
WHY IT MATTERS

Benefits of Robotics in Healthcare

01

Greater Precision

Robots can perform carefully controlled movements in tasks where precision is important.

02

Improved Efficiency

Automation can reduce repetitive workloads and support smoother healthcare operations.

03

Better Accessibility

Remote technologies can help connect healthcare professionals and patients across distances.

04

Support for Professionals

Robots can act as assistants, allowing healthcare workers to focus on tasks requiring human judgement.

THE BIG IDEA

Robots Don't Replace
Human Care.

Healthcare is built around human connection. Robotics can support doctors, nurses and therapists by handling specific tasks while people remain at the centre of care.
