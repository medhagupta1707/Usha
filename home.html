01 / UNDERSTANDING ROBOTICS

What is Healthcare Robotics?

Healthcare robotics combines robotics, artificial intelligence, sensors and medical technology to assist healthcare professionals and patients.

Robots can support tasks such as surgery, rehabilitation, diagnosis, hospital logistics and patient assistance.

HUMAN + MACHINE

Robots are designed to assist people and improve healthcare — not replace human care.

02 / IMPACT

How Robotics Helps Healthcare
